# Deck rubric

Grades ONE pitch-deck slide (1440×810 capture) or a 390 px phone capture of the deck (judge a phone capture
on the same criteria; stacked sections must read cleanly and nothing may be under ~16 px).

Score every criterion 0–10 using the anchors. Total = weighted mean. **Any KILL item caps the total at 5.**
9–10 = you would present it to a paying client with zero edits, and it looks like a shipped product from a
studio, not a generated mockup. "Clean but empty" is not premium — minimal restraint reads as unfinished.

### C1 Grounded visual (w=20)
Every slide carries real imagery from the client's world, a live device, or a drawn diagram with weight.
- 10: the client's own footage or photography full-bleed.
- 8: our live site inside laptop + phone chrome.
- 5: a correct photo used as wallpaper behind a text block.
- 2: a dark surface with hairlines and a text list — nothing to look at.

### C2 Composition is built for THIS slide, not a template (w=15)
- 10: devices, their site and ours arranged as one scene.
- 5: text-left/photo-right, or a row of identical cards.
- 2: generic headline-left/photo-right hero; stock dark dashboard.

### C3 Type and the one claim (w=15)
Huge condensed display type with character, ONE concrete claim, clear reading order. ≤30 words on the slide.
- 10: a giant claim that reads in one second.
- 5: polite heading sizes, every block the same weight, or 40+ words.
- 2: a script or paragraph pasted onto the slide.

### C4 Density without clutter (w=15)
Premium density: no empty bands, no dead quarter; breathable margins; nothing crammed.
- 10: every region earns its space.
- 5: a big empty band (top, bottom or one side) or one cramped corner.
- 2: a third of the slide is empty dark.

### C5 Proof is live or drawn (w=15)
Mechanics are drawn (call path, money flow), proof is live (iframe devices, real demo number/QR, real
transcript) — never a mock. A capture cannot show motion: a real site inside device chrome, a real phone
number or QR, or a real-call transcript COUNTS as live proof — score what is shown. If the brief says no
website exists, the demo phone line/QR is the live proof.
- 10: both their real site and ours live and scrollable side by side.
- 5: a static screenshot in device chrome, or a list where a diagram belongs.
- 2: an invented dashboard or mock UI presented as the product.

### C6 Brand and finish (w=10)
Client palette leads with one luminous accent; aligned edges, consistent margins, no clipping, legible at size.
- 10: the client's real palette, one accent event per slide, everything aligned.
- 5: default-looking palette or visible misalignment.
- 2: clipped/overlapping text, unreadable captions.

## KILL items (cap 5)
- Reads as a template / AI mockup: generic hero-left-text-right-image, identical card grids, stock-feeling copy.
- Content-swap of an earlier deck: same section order, hero mechanic and card grid with new copy/photos.
- Emoji or colour icons (icons are inline stroke SVG only). Navy+glass, neon, purple-pink-cyan gradients.
- Empty dark void (dark surface with only hairlines/grid). Text-only slide.
- Invented facts, fake stats, fake reviews, stock presented as their work, mock UI presented as real.
- Leftover content from another client.
- Clipped, overlapping, half-faded or unreadable text (including at 390 px).
- Wide/expanded grotesque as the headline face — display type must have character.

## What earns 8+
- Premium density: every slide carries imagery, a number, or an instrument.
- One luminous event per slide — a single bold accent, not scattered glow.
- Diagrams over prose for mechanics (money flow, call path, booking steps).
- Numbers with weight — huge honest numerals, never invented.
- Concrete headlines, never personified abstractions.
- Real brand palette from their logo/site; restrained two-tone accents.

## Live devices (binding)
- Every phone/laptop in the deck embeds the deployed site as a live iframe (390 px phone, 1440 px laptop,
  scaled). No static screenshots of our site in device frames.
- "Today" devices = a fresh real capture of their current site (or a live embed if frameable).
- Chat/transcript on a device = a REAL test of the demo line, labelled. Illustrative cards are labelled "Illustration".
- If a website exists: slide 2 is the live side-by-side (their site, our phone, our laptop, all scrollable),
  and the cover carries a working phone, not a wireframe panel. Bind deck keys inside same-origin frames.

## Interactives
- At least 3 interactive slides beyond the live iframes; fewer caps the deck at 6.
- Each must be specific to the prospect's business and change something they care about (a calculator from
  posted prices, a before/after slider, a step-through of their customer's flow) — parallax blobs, cursor
  trails and count-ups don't count.
- Each interactive: a one-line label of what is simulated, keyboard + touch operable, reduced-motion fallback.
